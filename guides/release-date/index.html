Demo notes

  • Your progress and character name carry into Early Access.
  • If you claim a name in the demo but do not log in between Nov 3, 2026 and Feb 3, 2027, the name is released.
  • Demo features: farming, decorating, sharing, salvaging, adventures and social play, plus the main questline in Loftia City.
  • Rewards include a Demo Banner, and a Pebble Chair / Pebble Couch for inviting 1 / 2 friends.
  • Early Access adds districts such as the Hanging Gardens (fishing, community farms) and Gridfall (salvaging).

FAQ

When does Loftia come out?

Loftia launches in Early Access on November 3, 2026 at 1 PM EST (18:00 UTC) on Steam and Epic Games Store for PC and Mac.

Is there a Loftia demo?

Yes. A free demo is live during Steam Next Fest, October 19 to 26, 2026. Progress and your character name carry over to Early Access.

Is Loftia coming to Switch, PS5, Xbox or mobile?

The developer has announced a Nintendo Switch version for 2027. No PS5, Xbox, iOS or Android release has been announced.

How much does Loftia cost?

The official FAQ lists a US Early Access base price of $29.99, with a limited-time 17% launch discount to $24.89 and regional pricing. An in-game shop sells optional cosmetics.
