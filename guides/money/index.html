How to Make Money in Loftia: Beginner Dew Guide

Dew is Loftia's currency. These tips are based on demo numbers; check them again once Early Access is live.

  1. Grow Tomatoes once you can. 15 Dew seed, 30 Dew sale, 15 minutes: roughly 60 Dew per hour per plot, double any other demo crop.
  2. Fill every plot. Income scales with plots, so the number of garden bed slots matters more than which of the 30 Dew/hour crops you pick.
  3. Mind the watering timer. Lettuce and Tomato want water every 5 minutes, Carrot, Potato and Cotton every 10. If you are away a lot, Potato needs the least attention per Dew.
  4. Cooking is not always better than selling raw. Community data shows Garden Salad (Tomato + Lettuce + Carrot) at the top of cooking profit, about 20 Dew over its ingredients; many dishes add only 10-17 Dew. Carrot Soup sells for 35 Dew but uses two Carrots worth 30.
  5. Open the Daily Goodies Chest. It can give free Lettuce, Carrot and Cotton seeds, which turns their sale into pure profit.
  6. Plan Farming perks. Fruitful Harvest (Lv 11) and Rapid Growth (Lv 16) both raise output; see the skill planner.
