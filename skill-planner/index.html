Loftia Skill Planner

Plan your perks across Loftia's seven skills: Farming, Cooking, Crafting, Foraging, Salvaging, Research and Community. Each skill goes to level 20. Tick the perks you want; the planner adds up points and warns you if a perk needs a higher level than you planned. Your plan is saved in your browser and can be shared as a link.

Which perks help farming income?

Fruitful Harvest (Farming Lv 11) and Rapid Growth (Farming Lv 16) raise crop output directly; toggle them in the crop calculator to see the effect. Batch Harvest saves clicks on full garden beds.
