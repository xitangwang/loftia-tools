About Loftia

Loftia is a cozy multiplayer game from Qloud Games set on floating sky islands, where you farm, salvage, craft, cook, decorate and go on adventures with friends. The Steam Next Fest demo runs Oct 19-26, 2026, and Early Access opens on November 3, 2026 on Steam and Epic (PC/Mac).

FAQ

What is the best crop for money in the Loftia demo?

Based on demo values, Tomato gives the most Dew per hour from a single plot: it sells for 30 Dew, its seed costs 15 Dew and it grows in 15 minutes, so about 60 Dew per hour if you replant right away. The other four demo crops each come out around 30 Dew per hour.

Does the calculator include seasons?

Not yet. No season rules for crops have been confirmed in the demo, so seasons are listed as TBD. You can edit any value in the table.

Will these numbers be the same in Early Access?

Not guaranteed. The data was recorded from the Steam Next Fest demo (Oct 19-26, 2026). Early Access starts November 3, 2026, and we will update the table after launch.

How many crops do you get per harvest?

This is not confirmed by our sources, so the calculator defaults to 1 per harvest. Change the Yield column if you see a different number in game.
